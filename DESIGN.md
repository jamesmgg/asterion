# Asterion design direction

A personal orbital observatory: the planet is the centerpiece and the instrument panel is quiet. Midnight ocean #050b16, observatory glass #101c2c, ice #c6eeff, sunlight #efb975, white #f3f7fb, muted slate #91a3b9. Outfit for large planetary names and brand; DM Sans for controls and prose. Both bundled locally.

Desktop: a small left observation column, dominant central globe, narrow impact instrument at right, chronological transport and horizontal planet dock along the bottom. Mobile: the planet remains visible above a collapsible bottom instrument, with a horizontally scrollable destination dock. No page scrolling required for navigation; the instrument itself scrolls.

The distinguishing move is a richly lit planet with restrained orbital graphics, rather than a dashboard of boxed statistics. Science controls are physical quantities with units. Time, display scale, and visual effect exaggeration are explicitly identified. Inspect the actual WebGL rendering and mobile touch targets, not just the surrounding layout.
